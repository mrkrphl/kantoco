# Cavite Gown Rentals — sample assets

Photos are face-free crops from Cavite Gown Rentals' public Facebook page
(facebook.com/CaviteGownRentals) and related public listing stills, prepared
for this private KantoCo sample only.

- `gown-red-*.webp` — red sequin evening gown (cutouts / bodice / skirt crops; no faces). Retired in r7.9, unused.
- `gown-blue-*.webp` — icy blue ball gown on a headless mannequin (FB photo grid crops). Retired in r7.9, unused.
- `photos/cgr-gown-*.webp` — r7.9 whole gown photos; sources in `photos/SOURCES.md`.
- `accessories-tiaras*.webp` — tiara display crops (person and payment QR cropped out)
- `accessory-tiara-ruby.webp`, `accessory-tiara-rose.webp` — single-tiara crops from `accessories-tiaras-2.webp` (no faces)
- `accessory-choker.webp`, `accessory-bracelet.webp` — accessory cutouts

Prices, sizes and booked dates in the sample app are **mock / sample data**.
Public listings did not publish rental rates.

No shop contact. Unlisted, noindex preview only.
